You can filter the dashboard by an event. Choose Event in the filter to see the visitors who did it, or "is not" for the visitors who did not. Everything they did that day stays in view, such as where they came from and which pages they read. A funnel's page, Journeys and Settings → Export take the same filter, and an outbound link works as a filter too.
