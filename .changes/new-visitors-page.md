The Visitors page now shows each visitor on a row of their own, with their day drawn as marks: a dot for each page they read and a circle where something happened, solid orange for one of your own events. The first visitor is open when you arrive, and you can press any other to see their visits step by step, where they came from and on what device, with links to the dashboard and Journeys for visitors like them. Four figures over the list compare the period with the one before, Converted counts the visitors who fired one of your named events, and tabs narrow the list to the visitors who converted, went past their first page or left after one.
