The Pages page now shows every page in one list, with a bar chart of its views and its change against the period before. The first page is open when you arrive, and you can press any other page to open it in place and see its trend, each figure against the period before, and how far its visitors scroll by device, source and country. The page has the dashboard's filter, and Today and Yesterday read by the hour.
