The new dashboard is out of beta and on for every team. Sources sit beside Pages, every block is the same size, and the filter and the date range stay in view while you scroll. The filter is one search field: type a country, a page or a source, then press Enter to add it, or Alt and Enter to leave it out (Option and Enter on a Mac). Outbound links are a tab of the Pages block. The page ends with a chart of your funnel and a map of the paths visitors take, beside the hours your site is busiest, and three small tiles for search clicks, uptime and performance. Scroll depth and the chart's choice between hours and days are no longer part of the dashboard.
