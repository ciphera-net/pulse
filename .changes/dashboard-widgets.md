You can now arrange your site's dashboard. Press the sliders icon at the right end of the toolbar to move widgets, change their size or remove them, and to add small widgets from other pages, such as Uptime, Search clicks, Funnels and Visitors now. The layout belongs to your team: everyone on the team and your share link see the same dashboard, anyone who can edit the site can change it, and Reset brings back the standard one. It is in beta for teams with beta features on in Settings → Team.
