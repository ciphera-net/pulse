With two or more filters on, the dashboard can now count what fits any one of them instead of all of them. Press the word "all" on the filters box and it reads "any of 2 filters": "Country is Norway" with "Device is mobile" then shows the visitors from Norway together with the visitors on a phone, and the choice is kept in the page's address and in a saved filter. Turn on beta features in Settings → Team to try it.
