In the new dashboard, the filter is one search field. Type what you are looking for, such as a country or a page, then press Enter to add it, or Alt and Enter to leave it out (Option and Enter on a Mac). Press F to open it from anywhere on the page. With two or more filters on, they fold into one box, so the date range stays where it is.
