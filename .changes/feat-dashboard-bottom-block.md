The new dashboard now ends with a chart of your funnel and a map of the paths visitors take through your site, beside the hours your site is busiest, with search clicks, uptime and performance in three small tiles. Press the chart, the map or a tile to open its page, and pick another funnel with the button beside the tabs. Turn on beta features in Settings → Team to try it.
