The Journeys page now draws where visitors went step by step, with each page's count beside it and how many visitors left the site after every step. Press a page or a link to keep its journeys highlighted and see under the chart how many of its visitors went on and where most of them went next, or press "Other pages" to draw its ten busiest pages into the chart. Turn on beta features in Settings → Team to try it.
