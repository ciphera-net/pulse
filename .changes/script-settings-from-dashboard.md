Your tracking script is now one line for every site, and your settings in Settings → General apply to it on their own. A change to scroll depth, outbound links, file downloads, clicks, copies and forms, Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control takes effect within a few minutes of saving, and the script on your site stays as it is. If you added options such as data-no-outbound to your script by hand, set them in Settings → General instead, because the script no longer reads them.
